Wellness Protocol Builder – Functional Requirements & User Flow

Questionnaire Access & Format
The Wellness Protocol Builder should be accessed via a dedicated link. Upon clicking the link, users are guided through a step-by-step questionnaire that presents one question per screen to ensure clarity, focus, and ease of completion.

Each screen must include a “Next” button to advance to the following question.

Link Display Content (Landing Section)

Title:
Wellness Protocol Builder

Description:
Complete your symptom assessment and generate a personalized wellness support plan.
Comprehensive detoxification and whole-body strategies developed by certified practitioners.

Section Header:
Body Health Assessment

Subtext:
Evaluate your current health status across key wellness indicators.

A simple, professional wellness-related icon or graphic should accompany this section.

Questionnaire Instructions

Instruction Text:
Please select the symptoms you experience on a daily basis.

Questionnaire Structure (One Question Per Screen)

Digestive Health

Energy & Cognition

Skin & Allergies

Sleep & Mood

Appetite & Weight

Pain & Physical Function

Exposure History

After completing each section, the user selects “Next” to proceed.
After the seventh section, the user selects “Submit.”

Post-Submission Logic & Output

Upon submission, the system analyzes responses and categorizes symptoms into three levels:

High Priority

Medium Priority

Low Priority

Personalized Protocol Delivery

The user is then presented with a customized wellness protocol, beginning with High Priority symptoms, followed by Medium and Low.

For each High Priority symptom category, the protocol must include:

Education

A brief explanation of the symptom pattern and underlying wellness implications.

Why It Matters

Clear reasoning on how this symptom impacts overall health, energy, metabolism, or detoxification capacity.

Supplement Recommendations

Targeted supplements aligned to the symptom profile.

Clear dosage guidelines.

Timing instructions (when to take).

Instructions on whether supplements should be taken with food, without food, or at specific times of day.

Next-Step Guidance

Actionable recommendations outlining what the client should focus on first.

Direction for ongoing support or practitioner follow-up, if applicable.
